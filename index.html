🤫 VISION — not yet built

Whisper Market — Private Prediction Markets

Current prediction markets expose every bet publicly — your position, your stake, your strategy, all visible before settlement.

With Flare Confidential Compute, users could bet privately on elections, sports, crypto prices, and AI outcomes — with nobody able to see your position, stake, or strategy until settlement. The first genuinely private prediction market.

Why it's not built yet: this depends on the same Flare Confidential Compute infrastructure that PortfolioVault's private valuation layer needs — currently gated behind credentials only Flare's team can issue.

🤖 VISION — not yet built

FlareBox AI — Sell AI Without Revealing It

Companies with valuable trading models, research models, or proprietary AI won't share them — and users don't want to reveal what they're asking either.

Upload a model into a Flare Confidential Compute enclave. Users send encrypted prompts. The model executes privately inside the enclave, and results return — the model is never exposed, and neither is the user's query. Two-way confidentiality, verified by hardware rather than by trust.

Why it's not built yet: same FCC dependency as Whisper Market above — a real, working idea waiting on infrastructure outside this project's control.
